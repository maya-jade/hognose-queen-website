About Me

Hi, I'm the Hognose Queen. Welcome to my site and thank you so much for your support! The furry guy is my boss, Void.

Here on HognoseQueen.com, what you see is what you get. I do not alter any of my photos. If you see anyone using my photos, please let me know as I take those matters very seriously!

Customer happiness is my top priority, so I would never sell you an animal that I wouldn't purchase myself. Every animal I list for sale is 100% healthy and has consistently been eating unscented prey items for at least a full month. I will not hold or take deposits on animals that are not eating properly or have any kind of health issues. If an animal is pet-only (aka special needs) it will be made clear in the description.

It is my guarantee that any snake you receive from me will not have mites, crypto, parasites, or any other major health issues due to my extremely high cleaning standards, strict quarantine practices, and periodic health testing.

Even if you don't shop with me, I am always here to answer any questions you may have so please feel free to reach out to me at your convenience via Facebook Messenger or Instagram Messages.

You can also send us a message! Please allow 24-48 hours for a response. (Disclaimer: I am not a professional!) I can only give advice and recommendations based off experience.
