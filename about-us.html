About Me

Hi, I'm the Hognose Queen. Welcome to my site and thank you so much for your support! The furry guy is my boss, Void.

Here on HognoseQueen.com, what you see is what you get. I do not alter any of my photos. If you see anyone using my photos, please let me know as I take those matters very seriously!

Customer happiness is my top priority, so I would never sell you an animal that I wouldn't purchase myself. Every snake must be consistently eating unscented prey items before being listed. I personally guarantee that any snake you receive from me will not have any mites, crypto, parasites, or any other major health issues due to the size of my collection and sanitation practices. I have a dedicated vet for all of my animals and I work from home so they are always under my care. I have one season per year (Spring - Summer) and I only breed a few of my females per season to extend their lives and ensure higher quality.

For your reference, I also care for many other species of reptiles and will be more than happy to answer any questions you may have regarding husbandry, feeding, etc. (Disclaimer: I am not a professional, I can only give advice and recommendations based on experience so I will redirect you to a veterinarian as needed)

Even if you don't shop with me, I am always here to answer any questions so please feel free to reach out to me at your convenience via my website, Facebook messenger or Instagram messages. Please allow 24-48 hours for a response.

Happy Hogging! ❤️
